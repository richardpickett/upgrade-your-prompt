# P22 — The accept or reject is the whole prompt

Rung 10 practice.

After the system drafts a record or a product, your turn only accepts or rejects it. You do not restate the policy the draft was built from. This is the top of what a prompt can show, and only when the store already held that policy. A genuinely new fact still has to be said once, earlier.

If the draft is right, accept it and stop. If it is wrong, reject it and name the miss, then let the system redraft. Do not rewrite the draft yourself in the prompt.
